# Branding

## Product Positioning

CatalogAI is an AI Commerce Workspace for e-commerce businesses.

## Core Promise

Your AI Commerce Workspace.

Upload one product image and let AI generate everything needed to launch and market that product across multiple marketplaces.

## Messaging Principles

- Lead with business outcomes: faster launches, consistent catalog quality, channel-ready content, and less manual production work.
- Frame the product as a connected commerce workspace, not a single-purpose listing generator.
- Use studio language for creation surfaces and workspace language for navigation, history, and operations.
- Keep AI visible as the operating layer, while focusing copy on what sellers can publish, review, and reuse.
- Support Amazon, Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy, eBay, Daraz, and Generic Stores in marketplace messaging.

## Product Vocabulary

- Product Analysis -> Product Intelligence
- Generate Listing -> Listing Studio
- SEO -> SEO Studio
- Marketplace -> Marketplace Studio
- Lifestyle Images -> Creative Studio
- Marketing Copy -> Marketing Studio
- Dashboard -> AI Workspace
- History -> Workspace History

## Voice

Modern, calm, capable, and outcome-oriented. Copy should feel like a premium SaaS workspace for sellers, marketers, agencies, and commerce operators.
